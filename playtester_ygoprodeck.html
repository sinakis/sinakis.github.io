Card data and pictures come from YGOPRODeck. They are downloaded once, kept in this browser, and reused after that. Add decks with “Add .ydk…”, “Paste deck”, by dropping .ydk files on the page, or with “Load folder…”.
Custom cards are saved in the browser, and also in playtester_files/ (cards.json, pics/, decks/) inside the folder you pick with “Load folder…”. IDs can contain letters.
